# 👩‍💼 Employee Attrition Analysis Dashboard (2020)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition patterns at Zencom for the year 2020. The objective is to identify key factors contributing to staff turnover, such as job roles, education, business travel, departments, and demographic attributes. By leveraging this data, the dashboard provides actionable insights that help HR teams improve retention, optimize workforce planning, and enhance organizational culture.

---

## 🗂️ Data Source

The dataset used is a synthetic HR dataset reflecting organizational structure, employee demographics, job characteristics, and attrition outcomes. It includes information on age, department, education, travel frequency, salary, admission types, and job performance across 1,470 employees.

---

## 🛠️ Tools and Methodology

- **Microsoft Excel**: Used for visual analytics, and dashboard development.
- **Excel**: Used for early-stage data exploration and shaping.
- **Segmentation**: Employees are analyzed by age group, job role, education, and department for granular insights.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Structure

The dashboard is organized into several analytical views covering attrition rate, demographics, job roles, and operational trends.

---

### Organization Overview
- **Total Employees**: 1,470  
- **Attrition Rate**: 16.12% (237 employees left in 2020)  
- **Average Age**: 37 years  
- **Top Job Role**: Sales Executive (326 employees)  
- **Highest Attrition**: **25–34 age group**

---

###  Attrition by Department & Job Role
- **Top Departments by Attrition**:
  - Sales (66% of attrition)
  - Research & Development (30%)
- **Stable Department**: Human Resources (lowest attrition and lowest salary group)
- **High Attrition Roles**: Research Scientists, Lab Technicians, and Sales Executives

---

### Education & Salary Distribution
- **Education Fields** with High Attrition:
  - Life Sciences (89 exits)
  - Medical (63 exits)
- **Top Earners**: Life Sciences graduates ($3.9M total salary), yet exhibit high turnover
- **Lower Earners**: Human Resources ($540K), but most stable group

---

### Business Travel & Doctor Performance
- **Business Travel Distribution**:
  - Rarely travels: 1,043 employees
  - Frequently travels: 277 employees
- **Top Doctors by Patient Load**:
  - Michael Smith (27 patients)
  - Johnson and Smith appear repeatedly among top five
- **Elective, Emergency, and Urgent Admissions** are evenly spread across conditions

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🧑 Demographics
- **25–34 age group** has the highest attrition (112 exits), despite being the dominant workforce group
- Younger and early-career employees need focused engagement and development opportunities

### 💼 Departmental Trends
- **Sales and R&D** departments experience the highest turnover
- Job fatigue or unclear growth paths could be leading contributors

### 🎓 Education & Compensation
- **Higher salaries do not guarantee retention**—Life Sciences and Medical fields earn the most but still lose employees at high rates
- Fields with clear internal pathways, like HR, show strong stability

### ✈️ Travel & Lifestyle Impact
- Business travel frequency doesn’t directly correlate with attrition, but work-life balance and flexibility could be key factors for long-term retention

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Engage Early-Career Employees**
   - Launch career pathing, mentorship programs, and upskilling opportunities for 25–34 age group.

2. **Investigate Sales & R&D Attrition Drivers**
   - Conduct anonymous surveys or exit interviews to uncover dissatisfaction drivers in high-turnover departments.

3. **Balance Workload Among Key Roles**
   - Especially in Sales and Tech roles where performance pressure may lead to burnout.

4. **Revisit Retention Plans in Life Sciences & Medical**
   - Despite high compensation, satisfaction may be low. Introduce recognition programs and flexible work policies.

5. **Leverage Data to Inform HR Strategy**
   - Use performance metrics, attrition rates, and education background insights to optimize recruitment and promotion strategies.
